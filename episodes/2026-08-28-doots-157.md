## Key Concepts

The ZAO is a web3 music community and incubator where members build, ship, and graduate real products together. Everything it builds - from WaveWarZ (on-chain music battles) to ZAOstock (the festival) - serves one idea: artists owning their work.

The community is a home where standing is earned, not bought. It is not a token-first project - the community and the work come before any token.
