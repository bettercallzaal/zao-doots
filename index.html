What The ZAO is

The ZAO is a web3 music community and incubator where artists own their work.

Founded in 2024. It started as a gated Farcaster social client for its own community on Base and became a lab: members prototype projects side by side, and when something is ready it graduates to its own home. It runs like a label and an incubator at once. Standing is earned, not bought, through Respect, an onchain reputation built by contributing each week. WaveWarZ, live-traded music battles; ZAOstock, a festival. The same through-line runs through all of it: get the artist heard, and let them keep what they make.
